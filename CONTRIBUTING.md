# Contributing to ToolsAA Universal Toolkit

Thank you for your interest in expanding the ToolsAA Universal Human Civilization Toolkit!

## Our Guiding Mission
Our mission is simple: **Whatever tool a human needs in daily life, career, or engineering — one site solves it all, 100% client-side.**

## How to Suggest a New Tool
Whether it is:
* An everyday household conversion or culinary ratio;
* A niche financial retirement formula or tax model;
* A fitness metric or biometric baseline calculation;
* Or a complex cloud/networking engineering converter...

If it solves a genuine human problem and can be computed **100% client-side without uploading user data to a server**, we want it in ToolsAA!

### To Submit a Request:
1. Open a new [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/GGXDMSW/toolsaa-universal-toolkit/issues).
2. Label it as `tool-request`.
3. Provide the tool name, primary formulas/specifications, and expected inputs/outputs.

Our automated cluster pipeline will review, architect, and schedule the tool for production rollout!
